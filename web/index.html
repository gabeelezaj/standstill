Using your iPhone as the camera

macOS only offers the iPhone to a browser when the phone is in Apple's “magic pose”. All five have to be true at once:

  • landscape orientation
  • screen off and locked
  • resting still — not in your hand
  • rear camera unobstructed
  • near the Mac, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on

Prop it in a stand, lock it, leave it alone, then press ↻. Once it connects you can pick the phone up and move it freely — the pose is only needed to make the connection.

Still nothing? Some Chrome builds miss Continuity Camera even in the pose. Safari is reliable here — double-click Standstill (Safari).app.
